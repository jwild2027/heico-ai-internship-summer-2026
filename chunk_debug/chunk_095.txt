For federal employees and contractors, Personal Identity Verification (PIV), used in accordance 
with FIPS 201, may be required to achieve access control. Organizations may also consider one 
or more of these techniques when determining how to support local access controls within their 
environments. Supplemental guidance for access controls can be found in the following 
documents:  
• NIST SP 800-63-3, Digital Identity Guidelines  
• NIST SP 800-73-4, Interfaces for Personal Identity Verification  
• NIST SP 800-76-2, Biometric Specifications for Personal Identity Verification  
• NIST SP 800-78-4, Cryptographic Algorithms
