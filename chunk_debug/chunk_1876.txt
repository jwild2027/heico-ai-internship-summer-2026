REV. 5 SECURITY AND PRIVACY CONTROLS FOR INFORMATION SYSTEMS AND ORGANIZATIONS _________________________________________________________________________________________________ CHAPTER THREE PAGE 228 This publication is available free of charge from: https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-53r5 Related Controls: All XX-1 Controls, PL-4, PM-12, PS-6, PT-1. Control Enhancements: None. References: None. PS-9 POSITION DESCRIPTIONS Control: Incorporate security and privacy roles and responsibilities into organizational position descriptions. Discussion: Specification of security and privacy roles in individual organizational position descriptions facilitates clarity in understanding the security or privacy responsibilities associated with the roles and the role-based security and privacy training requirements for the roles.
