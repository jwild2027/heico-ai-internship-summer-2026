Standing Lenticular Altocumulus Clouds.
Standing lenticular altocumulus clouds are formed on the crests of waves created by barriers in the
wind flow. The clouds show little movement, hence the name standing. Wind, however, can be
quite strong blowing through such clouds. They are characterized by their smooth, polished edges.
