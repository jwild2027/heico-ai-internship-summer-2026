PRIVILEGED USERS Implement the following additional monitoring of privileged users: [Assignment: organization-defined additional monitoring]. Discussion: Privileged users have access to more sensitive information, including security- related information, than the general user population. Access to such information means that privileged users can potentially do greater damage to systems and organizations than non-privileged users. Therefore, implementing additional monitoring on privileged users helps to ensure that organizations can identify malicious activity at the earliest possible time and take appropriate actions.
