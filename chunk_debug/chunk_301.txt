Managing External Pressures
Management of external pressure is the single most important key to risk management because
it is the one risk factor category that can cause a pilot to ignore all the other risk factors.
The use of personal standard operating procedures (SOPs) is one way to manage external
pressures. The goal is to supply a release for the external pressures of a flight.
