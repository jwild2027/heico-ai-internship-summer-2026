IA-7, SC-12, SC-13. (8) ACQUISITION PROCESS | CONTINUOUS MONITORING PLAN FOR CONTROLS Require the developer of the system, system component, or system service to produce a plan for continuous monitoring of control effectiveness that is consistent with the continuous monitoring program of the organization. Discussion: The objective of continuous monitoring plans is to determine if the planned, required, and deployed controls within the system, system component, or system service continue to be effective over time based on the inevitable changes that occur.
