As workload increases, attention cannot be devoted to several tasks at one time, and the pilot
may begin to focus on one item. When a pilot becomes task saturated, there is no awareness of
input from various sources, so decisions may be made on incomplete information and the
possibility of error increases.
When a work overload situation exists, a pilot needs to stop, think, slow down, and prioritize. It is
important to understand how to decrease workload.
