(6) SECURITY AND PRIVACY ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES | MINIMIZED SHARING Implement the security design principle of minimized sharing in [Assignment: organization-defined systems or system components]. Discussion: The principle of minimized sharing states that no computer resource is shared between system components (e.g., subjects, processes, functions) unless it is absolutely necessary to do so.
