REPEATABLE AND DOCUMENTED PROCEDURES Implement the security design principle of repeatable and documented procedures in [Assignment: organization-defined systems or system components]. Discussion: The principle of repeatable and documented procedures states that the techniques and methods employed to construct a system component permit the same component to be completely and correctly reconstructed at a later time. Repeatable and documented procedures support the development of a component that is identical to the component created earlier, which may be in widespread use.
