NIST Internal or Interagency Reports  
NIST IR series documents are reports on research findings, including background information 
for FIPS and SPs.  
https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/ir  
The following NIST IR documents are applicable to the OT security community: 
• NIST IR 7628, Rev. 1, Guidelines for Smart Grid Cybersecurity  
• NIST IR 8011 Vol. 1, Automation Support for Security Control Assessments: Volume 1: 
Overview  
• NIST IR 8011 Vol. 2, Automation Support for Security Control Assessments: Volume 2: 
Hardware Asset Management  
• NIST IR 8011 Vol. 3, Automation Support for Security Control Assessments: Software
