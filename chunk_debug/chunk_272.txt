D.1.5. International Society of Automation (ISA)  
The International Society of Automation (ISA) is a non-profit professional association founded 
in 1945 to create a better world through automation. ISA advances technical competence by 
connecting the automation community to achieve operational excellence. It is the trusted 
provider of standards-based foundational technical resources, driving the advancement of 
individual careers and the overall profession. ISA
