| PROCEDURAL RIGOR Implement the security design principle of procedural rigor in [Assignment: organization- defined systems or system components]. Discussion: The principle of procedural rigor states that the rigor of a system life cycle process is commensurate with its intended trustworthiness. Procedural rigor defines the scope, depth, and detail of the system life cycle procedures. Rigorous system life cycle procedures contribute to the assurance that the system is correct and free of unintended functionality in several ways.
