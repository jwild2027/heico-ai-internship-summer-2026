Acute fatigue can be prevented by proper diet and adequate rest and sleep. A well-balanced diet
prevents the body from needing to consume its own tissues as an energy source. Adequate rest
maintains the body’s store of vital energy.
Chronic fatigue, extending over a long period of time, usually has psychological roots, although an
underlying disease is sometimes responsible. Continuous high-stress levels produce chronic fatigue.
