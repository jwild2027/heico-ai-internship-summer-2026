Chapter 11:
Airport Operations
Introduction
The definition for airports refers to any area of land or water used or intended for landing or takeoff of
aircraft. This includes, within the five categories of airports listed below, special types of facilities
including seaplane bases, heliports, and facilities to accommodate tilt rotor aircraft. An airport includes
an area used or intended for airport buildings, facilities, as well as rights of way together with the
buildings and facilities.
