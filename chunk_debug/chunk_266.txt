Chapter 10:
Aeronautical Decision-Making and Judgment
Introduction
Aeronautical decision-making (ADM) is decision-making in a unique environment—aviation. It is a
systematic approach to the mental process used by pilots to consistently determine the best course of
action in response to a given set of circumstances. It is what a pilot intends to do based on the latest
information he or she has.
