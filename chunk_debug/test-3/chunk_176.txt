Any aircraft, within the limits of its structure,
may be stalled at any airspeed. When a sufficiently high AOA is imposed, the smooth flow of air over
an airfoil breaks up and separates, producing an abrupt change of flight characteristics and a sudden
loss of lift, which results in a stall.
