Obstacles to Maintaining Situational Awareness
Fatigue, stress, and work overload can cause a pilot to fixate on a single perceived important item
and reduce an overall situational awareness of the flight. A contributing factor in many accidents is a
distraction that diverts the pilot’s attention from monitoring the aircraft.
