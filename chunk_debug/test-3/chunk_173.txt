Load Factors in Steep Turns
At a constant altitude, during a coordinated
turn in any aircraft, the load factor is the
result of two forces: centrifugal force and
weight. [Figure 4-1] For any given bank
angle, the rate-of-turn varies with the
airspeed—the higher the speed, the slower
the rate-of-turn (ROT). This compensates for
added centrifugal force, allowing the load
factor to remain the same.
