Muscle spasms
The treatment for hyperventilation involves restoring the proper carbon dioxide level in the body.
Breathing normally is both the best prevention and the best cure for hyperventilation. In addition to
slowing the breathing rate, breathing into a paper bag or talking aloud helps to overcome
hyperventilation. Recovery is usually rapid once the breathing rate is returned to normal.
