Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts (TAF)
A TAF is a report established for the five statute mile radius around an airport. TAF reports are
usually given for larger airports. Each TAF is valid for a 24 or 30-hour time period and is updated four
times a day at 0000Z, 0600Z, 1200Z, and 1800Z. The TAF utilizes the same descriptors and
