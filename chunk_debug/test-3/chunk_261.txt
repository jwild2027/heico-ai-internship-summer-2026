While experiencing a hangover, a pilot is still under
the influence of alcohol. Although a pilot may think he
or she is functioning normally, motor and mental
response impairment is still present. Considerable
amounts of alcohol can remain in the body for over 16
hours, so pilots should be cautious about flying too soon after drinking.
