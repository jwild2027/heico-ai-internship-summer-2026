Fuel burn is
the most common weight change that takes place during flight. As fuel is used, the aircraft
becomes lighter and performance is improved, but this could have a negative effect on
balance. In small UA operations, weight change during flight may occur when expendable
items are used on board (e.g., a jettisonable load).
