Aviation Forecasts
Observed weather condition reports are often used in the creation of forecasts for the same area. A
variety of different forecast products are produced and designed to be used in the preflight planning
stage. The printed forecasts that pilots need to be familiar with are the terminal aerodrome forecast
(TAF), aviation area forecast (FA), inflight weather advisories (Significant Meteorological Information
(SIGMET), Airman’s Meteorological Information (AIRMET)), and the winds and temperatures aloft
forecast (FB).
