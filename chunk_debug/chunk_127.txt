The most severe type of low-level wind shear, a microburst, is associated with convective precipitation
into dry air at cloud base. Microburst activity may be indicated by an intense rain shaft at the surface
but virga at cloud base and a ring of blowing dust is often the only visible clue. A typical microburst has
a horizontal diameter of 1–2 miles and a nominal depth of 1,000 feet.
