6.1.1.2. 
Network Architecture Documentation (Supports the Outcome of 
ID.AM)  
Network architecture documentation tools help a manufacturer identify, document, and diagram 
the interconnections between networked devices, corporate networks, and other external 
connections. A comprehensive understanding of the interconnections within the environment is 
critical for the successful deployment of cybersecurity controls. This information is equally 
important for effective network monitoring.
