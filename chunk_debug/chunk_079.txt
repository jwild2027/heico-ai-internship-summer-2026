6.1.1.1. 
Mapping Data Flows (ID.AM-3)  
Data flow diagrams help a manufacturer understand the flow of data between networked 
components. Documenting data flows enables organizations to understand the expected behavior 
of their networks. This understanding of how devices communicate assists with troubleshooting 
as well as response and recovery activities. This information can be leveraged during forensic 
activities or used for analysis to identify anomalies.
