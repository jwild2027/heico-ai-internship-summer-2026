Human Factors
Why are human conditions, such as fatigue, complacency and stress, so important in aviation? These
conditions, along with many others, are called human factors. Human factors directly cause or
contribute to many aviation accidents and have been documented as a primary contributor to more
than 70 percent of aircraft accidents.
