BIOMETRIC AUTHENTICATORS Employ presentation attack detection mechanisms for biometric-based authentication. Discussion: Biometric characteristics do not constitute secrets. Such characteristics can be obtained by online web accesses, taking a picture of someone with a camera phone to obtain facial images with or without their knowledge, lifting from objects that someone has touched (e.g., a latent fingerprint), or capturing a high-resolution image (e.g., an iris pattern).
