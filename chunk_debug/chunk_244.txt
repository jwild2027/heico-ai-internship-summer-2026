Dehydration
Dehydration is the term given to a critical loss of water from the body. Causes of dehydration are
hot temperatures, wind, humidity, and diuretic drinks—coffee, tea, alcohol, and caffeinated soft
drinks. Some common signs of dehydration are headache, fatigue, cramps, sleepiness, and dizziness.
