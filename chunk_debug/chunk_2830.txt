Isolating system components with boundary protection mechanisms provides the capability for increased protection of individual system components and to more effectively control information flows between those components. Isolating system components provides enhanced protection that limits the potential harm from hostile cyber- attacks and errors. The degree of isolation varies depending upon the mechanisms chosen.
