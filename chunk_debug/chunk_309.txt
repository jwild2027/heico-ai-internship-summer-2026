Guidelines  
The NIST SP 800 series on information technology reports on the NIST ITL research, guidance, 
and outreach efforts in computer security and its collaborative activities with industry, 
government, and academic organizations. Focus areas include cryptographic technology and 
applications, advanced authentication, public key infrastructure, internetworking security, criteria 
and assurance, and security management and support.  
https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/sp800
