Continuous monitoring can be achieved using automated tools, through 
passive scanning, or with manual monitoring performed at a frequency 
deemed commensurate with the risk. For example, a risk assessment may 
determine that the logs from isolated (i.e., non-networked), non-critical 
devices should be reviewed monthly by OT personnel to determine 
whether anomalous behavior is occurring. Alternatively, a passive 
network monitor might be able to detect vulnerable network services 
without having to scan the devices.
