3. Operation
A. Extension and stowage of the Snack Folding Table
(1) The snack folding table is secured in the stowed position by a latch located at the center of

the backrest, just above the upper edge of the table. This latch must be released to extend
the snack folding table.

(2) The movable armrests can be stowed between two backrests by pressing the lock located on
the bottom of the armrest, near the backrest.
