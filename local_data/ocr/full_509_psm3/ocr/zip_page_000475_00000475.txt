REPAIRS

1. General

A. The repairs herein described allow returning the equipment to service in the same conditions as
originally.

B. This section describes repairs made using room-temperature cure materials (wet lay-up).

NOTE: - The repairs called for in this section are wet lay-up repairs. These repairs require
rapid use of catalyzed resin materials.

- Wet lay-up will not restore the structure to its original strength and durability. A peri-
odic inspection plan for the repair area may be required.

- The repairs in this section are room-temperature repairs, the cure of which may be
accelerated by the application of heat as specified herein.

2. Precautions When Carrying Out Repairs

WARNING: - HEAT, FIRE OR SPARKS CAN CAUSE AN EXPLOSION. USE EXPLOSION PROOF
EQUIPMENT WHEN PERFORMING THESE REPAIRS. NON COMPLIANCE COULD
CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONNEL.

- BREATHING VAPORS OR ALLOWING SOLVENT TO CONTACT SKIN OR EYES IS
HAZARDOUS. WEAR NEOPRENE GLOVES WITH COTTON LINERS, PROTEC-
TIVE CLOTHING AND EYE GOGGLES.

- IF CHEMICAL CONTACT OCCURS, WASH THOROUGHLY WITH WATER. IF
CHEMICAL SHOULD SPLASH INTO EYES, FLUSH EYES WITH LARGE QUANTI-
TIES OF WATER AND SEEK MEDICAL AID. USE MECHANICAL VENTILATION OR
RESPIRATORY PROTECTION WHEN WORKING IN A CONFINED SPACE OR
AREA.

A. Perform the repair procedures specified herein only in areas of reasonable ventilation. Areas
containing oil mist, exhaust fumes, gases, soot, rain, dust, or other matter are specifically prohib-
ited.

B. Protect the surfaces from contamination and do no touch cleaned parts or adhesive with bare
hands. Use clean white gloves when handling the parts.

3. Equipment

A. Vacuum systems should be capable of maintaining a minimum of 22 inches of mercury
(0.745 bars).
