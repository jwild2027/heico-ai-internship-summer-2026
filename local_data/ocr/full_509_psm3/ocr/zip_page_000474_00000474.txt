MAINTENANCE MANUAL WITH
ILLUSTRATED PARTS LIST

CHECK

1. General
A. The following checks must be carried out on the passenger seats:
(1) Visually check all bolts, screws, and nuts removed for damaged threads and/or heads.
(2) Visually check the bushings removed for chafing.

(3) Visually check the parts for nicks, cracks, cuts, corrosion, scoring, excessive wear, and
defects that could impair an efficient seat operation.
