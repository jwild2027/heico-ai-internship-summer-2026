DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION

1. General

The passenger seats are arranged into two rows in the aircraft, one comprising single seats and
another comprising double seats. In some configurations, the last row may also comprise triple
seats.

2. Description

A.

The Single Passenger Seat (Figure 1) configurations listed below may be found on the "EMB-
120" Brasilia:

(1) First row single passenger seat with plug-in type snack table.

(2) Complete Single Passenger Seat with two armrests and snack folding table.

(3) Single Passenger Seat without armrest on the wall side (emergency exit position).
(4) Single Passenger Seat without snack folding table (last row).

(5) Single Passenger Seat with longer armrest, without snack folding table, to fit the triple pas-
senger seat row.

The Double Passenger Seat (Figure 2) may be found in the following configurations:
(1) First row Double Passenger Seat with plug-in type snack table.

(2) Complete Double Passenger Seat with armrests and snack folding table.

(3) Double Passenger Seat without armrest on the wall side (emergency exit position).
(4) Double Passenger Seat without snack folding table (last row).

There is only one configuration for the Triple Passenger Seat (Figure 3), with three armrests and
without snack folding table.

The composite structure of the seat supports the armrests, the backrest and the snack folding
table. The legs are mechanically attached to the seat structure.

The center and aisle armrests consist of a composite structure covered with thermoplastic mate-
rial, a self-skinning foam cushion and a metal lining.

It is designed, as required, with an ashtray and it can be pivoted upward to be stowed between
the two adjacent backrests.

The armrest of the wall side has a similar design, except that it is mounted in a fixed position.
