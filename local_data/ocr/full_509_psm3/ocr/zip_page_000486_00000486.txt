B. Double Passenger Seat Leg Repair.

(1) Repair Materials.

PART QTY. MATERIAL
1 | LATERAL STRUCTURE 1 -
(P/N 120-29074-001)

2 | REPAIR DOUBLER 2 | ALCLAD PLATE 2024-T3
1.016 mm (0.040 in)

3 | ORIGINAL REINFORCEMENT DOUBLER 2 -

4 | FASTENER (CR3242-4) OR MS20470 AD4 AR -

5 | FASTENER (CR3212-4) OR MS20470 AD4 AR -

NOTE: Ifthe hole is damaged, use the oversize fastener (5) listed above.

(2) Repair Procedure.

(a) Remove the original reinforcing doublers (3) from the leg. Refer to SRM (Structural
Repair Manual) - chapter 51-40-02 for Fastener Installation/Removal.

(b) Inspect the leg for cracks. If there is any crack in the leg, locate the end of the crack and

drill a stop hole of 3.175 mm (1/8 in).

(c) Smooth all dents and deformation on the leg.

(d) Set the repair doublers (2) over the leg and reinforcing doublers (3) over the repair dou-
bler, drilling them using the holes in the reinforcing doublers as a guide, and the new
ones to fix them together. Refer to SRM chapters: 51-25-00 for Drilling, 51-40-04 for
Fastener Hole Sizes, 51-40-05 for Fastener Edge Margin, and 51-70-05 for Typical

Repair Instructions of Formed Part.

(e) Attach the repair doublers (2) and reinforcing doubler (3) to the leg using fasteners (4
and 5). Refer to SRM chapter 51-40-07 for Selection of Rivet Length.

EFFECTIVITY: ALL
