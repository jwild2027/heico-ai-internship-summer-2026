DISASSEMBLY

1. Tools Required

A.

Tools commonly used by the technician.

2. Disassembly

A.

Before starting the disassembly, carry out the testing procedures on the unit according to the
TESTS AND FAULT ISOLATION Section to locate and determine the extent of the malfunction.
Disassemble the unit just sufficiently to gain access to the faulty or defective part. The following
procedures impart directions for a possible complete disassembly.

NOTE: Do not disassemble parts unnecessarily.

Because of the similarities between the seat configurations covered in this manual and for expla-
nation purposes, where there is no reference to a specific configuration, the text is common to all
of them.

NOTE: All parts should be marked before disassembly in order to facilitate subsequent assembly
and to avoid assembly errors.

Seat Belt (60) of Single Seat Disassembly (detail D of figure 1 of the Illustrated Parts List)
(1) Remove nut (90), washers (70 and 80), bolts (110 and 120), and bushing (100).

Seat Belt (80) of Double Seat Disassembly (detail A of figure 29 of the Illustrated Parts List)
(1) On the boards:

(a) Remove nut (90), washers (110 e 115), bolts (120, and 125), and bushing (100).
(2) Between seats:

(a) Remove nut (90), washer (115), bolt (130), two bushings (100), and washer (115).
Seat Belt (80) of Triple Seat Disassembly (detail B of figure 76 of the Illustrated Parts List)
(1) On the boards:

(a) Remove nut (150), washer (120), bolts (90 and 110), and bushing (140).

(2) Between seats:

(a) Remove nut (150), washer (130), bolt (100) and two bushings (140).
