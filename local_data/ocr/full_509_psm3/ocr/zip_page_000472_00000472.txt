(3) Water Insoluble Stains - Category (3) of item 1.A.

(a) Soak the area with a chlorinated solvent directly to the dry fabric and, working with a
bone spatula remove the stains while the surface is wet.

(b) After the removal of the stains, clean the surface as described in items 2.A.(1) and
2.A.(2).

(4) Unknown Stains - Category (4) of item 1.A.

(a) There are many commercial removal kits that include stepwise cleaning procedures for
stains of unknown origin.

(b) These procedures should first be tried on a small area for colorfastness. Also the
reagents recommended by the kit should be checked to confirm that it is a nonionic
reagent.

NOTE: Anionic and alkaline reagents could impair the flame retardant characteristics of
the fabric.

(c) Once these items have been confirmed, follow the recommended procedures of the kit.
B. Seat Cover Removed - Dry Cleaning.

(1) Apply a matching-type Velcro fastener to all Velcro fasteners to prevent damage during the
cleaning.

(2) Covers should be turned inside out during the cleaning.

CAUTION: THE SOLUTION MUST BE COLD AND THE WATER CONTENTS MUST NOT BE
OF MORE THAN 8% TO PREVENT THE COVER FROM EXCESSIVE SHRINK-
AGE.

(3) Dry-clean with a mixture of solvent (per FED. SEC. P-D-680, Type 1), neutral detergent, and
cold water. The solution must be in the proportion recommended by the detergent manufac-
turer and the temperature must not be higher than 72°F (22°C).

(4) The loading capacity of the dry cleaning machine employed should be between 22 to 66 Ib
(10 and 30 kg). In machines with a loading capacity greater than 66 Ib (30 kg), the mechani-
cal action is such that accelerated abrasion of the fabric may occur.
