ILLUSTRATED PARTS LIST

F. Seat Backrest (30) Installation (figure 1 of the Illustrated Parts List).
(1) Position the Seat Backrest Lining (30) and press its Velcro fastener.
(2) Attach the Snack Table Cover Latch (250) by installing its three attaching screws (270).

(3) Position the Magazine Holder (200) by pressing its two attaching male fastener buttons and
the Velcro fastener.

G. Section PVC (160) Assembly (figure 1 of the Illustrated Parts List).

(1) Position the PVC section (160) and attach it by installing the screw (190), washer (170), and
nut (180).

H. Fixed Armrest Assembly (detail A of figure 1 of the Illustrated Parts List).
(1) Position the front trim cover (240) and attach it by installing the screw (270).
(2) Position the ashtray (210) into the front trim cover (240).

(3) Position the seat armrest lining assembly (220) and attach it by installing its washer (280),
screw (270), and nylon plug (260).

(4) Position the rear trim cover (230) and attach it by installing its screw (270).

J. Movable Armrest Assembly (detail B of figure 29 of the Illustrated Parts List).
(1) Position the front trim cover (250) and attach it by installing the screw (280).
(2) Position the ashtray (210) into the front trim cover (250).

(3) Position the seat armrest lining assembly (220) and attach it by installing its washer (290),
screw (280), and nylon plug (270).

(4) Position the front trim cover (240) and attach it by installing its screw (280).

K. Lateral Trim Cover Assembly - only for seats without armrest on the sidewall (figure 2 of the Illus-
trated Parts List).

(1) Position the lateral trim cover (250) and attach it by installing its screw (280).
L. Armrests (200) of the Single Seat Installation (detail E of figure 79 of the Illustrated Parts List).

(1) Position the armrest (200) in the seat structure and install the screw (210) and nut (220).
