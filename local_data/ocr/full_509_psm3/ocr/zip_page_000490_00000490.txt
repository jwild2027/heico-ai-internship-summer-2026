D. Precautionary Measures for Finish Topcoating.

WARNING: — PERSONNEL DEALING WITH PAINTING PRODUCTS MUST BE PROPERLY
PROTECTED BY WEARING GLOVES AND MASK.

— IF THE PAINT SPLASHES IN THE EYES, IT MUST BE FLUSHED OUT
PROMPTLY WITH RUNNING WATER. OBTAIN MEDICAL AID IMMEDIATELY.

E. Finish Topcoat - Apply Epoxy Paint.

Apply the epoxy paint according to the procedures described in CPM (Corrosion Prevention Man-
ual) chapter 51-21-06 for External Finish - Finish Topcoat - Cleaning/Painting.

F. Finish Topcoat - Apply Polyurethane Paint.

Apply the polyurethane paint according to the procedures described in CPM (Corrosion Preven-
tion Manual) chapter 51-21-06 for External Finish - Finish Topcoat - Cleaning/Painting.
