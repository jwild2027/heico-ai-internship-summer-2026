F. Life Jacket Stowage (130) Disassembly (detail C of figure 1 of the Illustrated Parts List)

(1) Remove the Life Jacket Stowage (130) by releasing its four male fastener buttons (150).
G. Seat Bottom (20) Removal (figure 01 of the Illustrated Parts List)

(1) Remove Seat Bottom (20) by loosing its retaining Velcro fastener.
H. Seat Backrest (30) Removal (figure 01 of the Illustrated Parts List)

(1) Remove the Magazine Holder (200) by loosing its two attaching male fastener buttons and
the Velcro fastener.

(2) Remove the Snack Table Cover Latch (250) by loosing its three attaching screws (270).
(3) Remove the Seat Backrest Lining (30) by loosing the Velcro fastener.
J. Section PVC (160) Removal (figure 01 of the Illustrated Parts List)
(1) Remove nut (180), washer (170), and screw (190), and detach the PVC section (160).
K. Fixed Armrest Disassembly (detail A of figure 01 of the Illustrated Parts List)
(1) Remove the rear trim cover (230) by removing its attaching screw (270).

(2) Remove the seat armrest lining assembly (220) by removing its attaching nylon plug (260),
screw (270), and washer (280).

(3) Disassemble the front trim cover (240) by removing the screw (270).
(4) Remove the ashtray (210) from the front trim cover (240).

L. Movable Armrest Disassembly (detail B of figure 29 of the Illustrated Parts List)
(1) Remove the rear trim cover (250) by removing its attaching screw (280).

(2) Remove the seat armrest lining assembly (220) by removing its attaching nylon plug (270),
screw (280) and washer (290).

(3) Disassemble the front trim cover (240) by removing the screw (280).
(4) Remove the ashtray (210) from the front trim cover (240).

M. Lateral Trim Cover (250) Removal - only for seats without armrest on the sidewall (figure 2 of the
Illustrated Parts List)

(1) Remove the attaching screw (280) and detach the lateral trim cover (250).
