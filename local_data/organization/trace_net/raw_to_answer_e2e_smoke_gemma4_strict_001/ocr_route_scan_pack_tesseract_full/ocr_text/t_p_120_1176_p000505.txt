ASSEMBLY
1. Tools Required
A. Tools commonly used by the technician.
2. Assembly
A. Seat Belt (60) of Single Seat Assembly (detail D of figure 1 of the Illustrated Parts List).
(1) Attach the seat belt (60) to the seat structure by installing bolts (110 and 120), bushing (100),
washers (70 and 80), and nut (90).
B. Seat Belt (80) of Double Seat Assembly (detail A of figure 29 of the Illustrated Parts List).
(1) On the boards:
(a) Attach the seat belt (80) to the seat structure by installing bolts (120 and 125), bushing
(100), washers (110 and 115), and nut (90).
(2) Between seats:
(a) Attach the seat belt (80) to the seat structure by installing the bolt (130), two bushings
(100), two washers (115), and nut (90).
C. Seat Belt (80) of Triple Seat Assembly (detail B of figure 76 of the Illustrated Parts List).
(1) On the boards:
(a) Attach the seat belt (80) to the seat structure by installing bolts (90 and 110), bushing
(140), washer (120), and nut (150).
(2) Between seats:
(a) Attach the seat belt (80) to the seat structure by installing the bolt (100), bushing (140),
washer (130), and nut (150).
D. Life Jacket Stowage (130) Assembly (detail C of figure 1 of the Illustrated Parts List).
(1) Position the Life Jacket Stowage (130) in place and press its four male fastener buttons
(150) into the support (140).
E. Seat Bottom (20) Installation (figure 1 of the Illustrated Parts List).
(1) Position the Seat Bottom (20) and press its Velcro fastener.
EFFECTIVITY: ALL
