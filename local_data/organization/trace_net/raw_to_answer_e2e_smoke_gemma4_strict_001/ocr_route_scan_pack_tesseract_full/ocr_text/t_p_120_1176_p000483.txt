B. Repair Instructions.

(1) Remove the armrest attaching parts from the seat structure: nut (240), washer (230), bush-
ing (220), and screw (210) (figure 87 of Illustrated Parts List).

(2) Using one of the three options, manually impregnate the metal repair part with epoxy resin
and attach it to the seat.

(3) Allow the repair to cure. Refer to SRM (Structural Repair Manual) chapter 51-70-01.

(4) Apply one fillet of the same resin to all the contour of the repair part.

(5) As an option, finish the reworked region with one coat of wash primer, epoxy primer and
polyurethane top coat . Refer to CPM (Corrosion Preventive Manual) chapters 51-21-03 Sur-
face Protection Methods, 51-21-04 Surface Passivation, and 51-21-05 Surface Protection.
NOTE: This painting will peel off in the spot where the metal latch touches the repair part.
