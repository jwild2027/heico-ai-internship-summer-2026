Repair as indicated in detail C of figure 614.

Use glass-bead blast over the area where the repair sleeve should be installed in the frame
(1) as well as in the repair sleeve (2) around the area where the weld will be applied.

Set the repair sleeve (2) over the metal seat back frame (1).

Weld around the extremity of the repair sleeve, using the TIG process with rod MIL-R-5632,
Class Il, Type | (3).

Use a stove to do a stress-relief locally, with the temperature between 995 and 1049°F (535
and 565°C) during 2 hours.

Sand smooth all rough surfaces.
Inspect the welded area for cracks using liquid dye-penetrant method (MIL-STD-6866).

Paint the repaired frame with epoxy primer and then apply a polyurethane top coat. Refer to
CPM (Corrosion Prevention Manual) chapters: 51-21-05 for External Finish - Final Surface
Protection, and 51-21-06 for External Finish - Finish Topcoat.
