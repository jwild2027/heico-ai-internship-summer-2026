C. Triple Passenger Seat Leg Repairs.
(1) Repair Materials.
PART MATERIAL
1 LATERAL STRUCTURE (P/N 120-29073- 1 -
015)
2 LATERAL LEG STRUCTURE (P/N 120- 1 -
29073-006)
3 REPAIR DOUBLER 2 ALCLAD PLATE 2024-T3
1.016 mm (0.040 in)
4 ORIGINAL REINFORCEMENT DOUBLER 2 -
5 FASTENER (CR3242-4) OR MS20470 AD4 AR -
6 FASTENER (CR3212-4) OR MS20470 AD4 AR -
NOTE: If the hole is damaged, use the oversize fastener (5) listed above.
(2) Repair Procedure.

(a) Remove the original reinforcing doublers (4) from the leg. Refer to SRM (Structural
Repair Manual) - chapter 51-40-02 for Fastener Installation/Removal.

(b) Inspect the leg for cracks. If there is any crack in the leg, locate the end of the crack and
drill a stop hole of 3.175 mm (1/8 in).

(c) Smooth all dents and deformation in the leg.

(d) Set the repair doublers (3) over the leg and reinforcing doublers (4) over the repair dou-
bler, drilling them using the holes in the reinforcing doublers as a guide, and the new
ones to fix them together. Refer to SRM chapters: 51-25-00 for Drilling, 51-40-04 for
Fastener Hole Sizes, 51-40-05 for Fastener Edge Margin, and 51-70-05 for Typical
Repair Instructions of Formed Part.

(e) Attach the repair doublers (3) and reinforcing doubler (4) to the leg using fasteners (5
and 6). Refer to SRM chapter 51-40-07 for Selection of Rivet Length.
