Base Resin Epoxy A, B, C, or D (Table 2 of SRM 51-70-01)

B. Repair Instructions.

(1) Remove the armrest attaching parts from the seat structure: nut (240), washer (230), bush-

(2) Remove the damaged part and delaminated plies according to the procedures described in
SRM (Structural Repair Manual) 51-70-01 Fiberglass / Aramid Fiber / Carbon Fiber Compos-
