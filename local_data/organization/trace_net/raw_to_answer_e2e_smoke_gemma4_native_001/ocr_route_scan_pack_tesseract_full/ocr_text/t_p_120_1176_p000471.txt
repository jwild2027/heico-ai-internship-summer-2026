2. Cleaning Procedures for Seat Cover
NOTE: When the fire-blocking fabric is dirty, perform a commercial dry cleaning, with solvent per
FED. SPEC. P-D-680, Type | or equivalent.
A. Seat Cover Installed.
(1) Collect all loose particles with a vacuum cleaner.
(2) Water Removable stains - Categories (1) and (2) of item 1.A.
WARNING: ALTHOUGH THE STAINS IN THIS CATEGORY ARE WATER SOLUBLE, IT IS
NOT RECOMMENDED THAT THE SEAT COVER BE LAUNDERED IN
WATER. LAUNDERING OF WOOL AND WOOL/NYLON BLENDS IN WATER
MAY CAUSE EXCESSIVE SKRINKAGE AND A LOSS IN THE FLAME-RETAR-
DANT PROPERTIES.
(a) Prepare the carpet cleaning solution according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
(b) Apply it onto the stain and, working with a spatula or using a soft-bristle brush, remove
the stain from the fabric, being careful not to soak the seat upholstery.
(c) Fully dry the surface with a cloth.
