ILLUSTRATED PARTS LIST
1. General
A. This section lists and illustrates all the applicable assemblies, subassemblies, and components.
2. Vendor's List
A. A list of names and addresses of vendors is supplied together with the relevant codes for refer-
ence in the Detailed Parts List. The list is arranged in numerical order, according to the vendor
codes. Vendor part numbers with no FEDERAL SUPPLY CODE or NATO SUPPLY CODE
assigned are therein identified with a code preceded by the capital letter "E".
3. Numerical Index
A. The numerical index contains every number that appears in the Detailed Parts List and is
arranged in dual columns, in alphanumerical sequence.
(1) PART NUMBER Column.
The part number starts at the extreme left hand, continuing to the right, one position at a
time.
The order of precedence due to the part number arrangement for the first position (extreme
left) is the following:
- Letters A thru Z
- Numerals 0 thru 9
For the second and subsequent positions, the following order of precedence is given:
- Space (blank column)
- Diagonal (slant)
- Dot
- Dash (-)
- Letters A thru Z
- Numerals 0 thru 9
NOTE: The letter O is always considered a numerical zero.
(2) CH-SEC - UN - FIG - ITEM Column.
This column lists the chapter, section, unit, figure, and item numbers of the applicable parts.
Where a part has not been assigned an item number, only the chapter, section, unit, and fig-
ure numbers will appear .
(3) AIRLINE STOCK NUMBER Column.
This column is intended for use by the Airlines for inclusion of their codes.
