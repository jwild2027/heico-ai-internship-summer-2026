ILLUSTRATED PARTS LIST

INTRODUCTION

This manual presents all the operations carried out in a workshop after the disassembly of the passen-
ger seats of the EMB-120 "BRASILIA" aircraft. It allows a technician not acquainted with the equipment
to restore it to its operational conditions.

This manual contains the component description and operation, detailed instructions for disassembling,
checking, repairing and reassembling the component, and an illustrated parts list.

All the procedures described in this manual have been performed by the manufacturer prior to its release
to the users.

The methods presented herein, especially those concerning repairs, must not be modified or extended
to other cases without prior authorization granted by the aviation authorities and the manufacturer.
