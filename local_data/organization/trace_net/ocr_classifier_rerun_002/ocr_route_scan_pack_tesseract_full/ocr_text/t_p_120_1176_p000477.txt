B. Determine Damage (figure 602).
CAUTION: CHEMICAL PAINT STRIPPERS WILL ATTACK RESIN SYSTEMS, AND SHOULD
NOT BE USED TO REMOVE PAINT BEFORE MAKING DAMAGE ASSESSMENT.

(1) Examine visually for extent of damage.

(2) Check panel in vicinity of damage for entry of water, oil, fuel, dirt, or other foreign matter.

(3) Check for delamination around the damage by tapping firmly on the skin with a small solid
metal disk. Tap uniformly, following parallel lines along the suspect area. If delamination is
present, a dull sound will be heard as opposed to a clear metallic ring on a solidly bonded
area. With the aid of a marker, the contour of the delaminated area may be determined by
marking the spots where the changes in sound occur (figure 603).

(4) Make a chamfer in crack region in order to get a bit of fiber-glass layers (style 7781).

C. Remove Damage and Repair Damaged Area.
(1) Remove damage as follows:
(a) Trim out the damaged lamination to a smooth shape with rounded corners, or a circular
or oval shape.
CAUTION: — SANDING FOR ADHESION OR FINISH REMOVAL MUST NOT EXPOSE OR
DAMAGE FIBERS WHICH ARE NOT IN THE UNTAPERED REPAIRED
AREA. LOSS OF STRUCTURAL STRENGTH WILL OCCUR IF THIS CAU-
TION IS NOT OBSERVED.
— DO NOT USE PAINT STRIPPER FOR THE REMOVAL OF FINISH OR DAM-
AGE TO RESIN WILL OCCUR.
(b) In areas where contamination cannot be removed by cleaning or drying, remove the con-
tamined structure along with other damage.

(2) Repair to Damaged Area (figure 603).

WARNING: — SANDING GIVES OFF A FINE DUST THAT MAY CAUSE SKIN IRRITATIONS.

BREATHING OF AN EXCESSIVE AMOUNT OF THIS DUST MAY BE INJURI-
OUS. OBSERVE PRECAUTIONS FOR SKIN AND WEAR RESPIRATION PRO-
TECTION.

— EXPLOSION-PROOF EQUIPMENT MUST BE USED WHERE THE POSSIBIL-
ITY OF VAPOR IGNITION EXISTS.

— THE USE OF ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT FOR GRAPHITE-FIBER COMPOSITE
MATERIAL CUTTING IS NOT RECOMMENDED, SINCE GRAPHITE IS A CON-
DUCTIVE MATERIAL.
