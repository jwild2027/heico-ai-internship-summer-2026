(7) Dry cleaning procedure:
(a) Dip - Wash stage (on high dip):

1 The upholstery is wet out in solvent with the addition of 3 to 5 g/l detergent. The addi-
tion of water is carried out via the hopper or automatic metering pump to the cage ina
mixture with detergent (or as recommended by the detergent manufacturer for the
product used). The solvent is circulated without going through the filter for 7 - 10 min-
utes, the solvent being then pumped to the still and the fabrics centrifuged.

(b) Filter - Rinse Stage:

1 Distilled solvent is introduced and continuously circulated for 3 - 5 minutes from the
cage via the button trap, pump, and filter before returning to the cage. This solvent is
discharged to the working tank for the use as the dip - wash part is the next cycle. The
fabrics are then centrifuged for 2 minutes and dried.
