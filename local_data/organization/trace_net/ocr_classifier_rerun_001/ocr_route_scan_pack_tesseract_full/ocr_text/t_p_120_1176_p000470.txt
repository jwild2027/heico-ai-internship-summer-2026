CAUTION: OBEY THE SAFETY PRECAUTION DURING THE CLEANING OPERATIONS BELOW:

MAKE SURE THAT THE CLEANING OPERATIONS ON ADJACENT SURFACES DO
NOT CAUSE THE SURFACES ALREADY CLEANED TO BECOME DIRTY AGAIN.

REMOVE THE STAINS IMMEDIATELY. THERE ARE STAINS THAT YOU CANNOT
REMOVE AFTER SOME TIME HAS ELAPSED.

DO NOT USE FLAMMABLE SOLVENTS ON THE SEAT.
DO NOT APPLY SPRAY SOLVENT.
KEEP THE SOLVENTS IN CLOSED CONTAINERS.

DO NOT USE METAL ACCESSORIES (STEEL WOOL, STEEL WIRE BRUSH, ETC.)
FOR THE CLEANING.

A. To clean flame-resistant upholstery of seat cover, wool and wool blends, carefully, follow the
instructions of item 2.

There are the following types of stains:

(1) Water soluble stains such as tea, coffee, red wine, fruit, urine, etc.

(2) Protein type water removable stains such as milk, blood, chocolate, etc.

(3) Water insoluble stains such as ball-point pen ink, lipstick, nail polish, shoe polish, etc.

(4) Unknown stains.
