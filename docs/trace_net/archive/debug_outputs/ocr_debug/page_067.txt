Decision-Making in a Dynamic Environment  
A solid approach to decision-making is through the use of analytical models, such as the 5 Ps, 3P, and 
DECIDE. Good decisions result when pilots gather all available information, review it, analyze the 
options, rate the options, select a course of action, and evaluate that course of action for correctness.  
In some situations, there is not always time to make decisions based on analytical decision-making 
skills. A good example is a quarterback whose actions are based upon a highly fluid and changing 
situation. He intends to execute a plan, but new circumstances dictate decision-making on the fly. This 
type of decision-making is called automatic decision-making or naturalized decision-making.
