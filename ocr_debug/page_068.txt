Automatic Decision-Making 
For the past several decades, research into how people actually make decisions has revealed that 
when pressed for time, experts faced with a task loaded with uncertainty first assess whether the 
situation strikes them as familiar. Rather than comparing the pros and cons of different approaches, 
they quickly imagine how one or a few possible courses of action in such situations will play out.
